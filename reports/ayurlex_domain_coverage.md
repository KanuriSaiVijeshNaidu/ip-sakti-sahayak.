## 2. Domain Specifications & Statutory Grounding

### Domain 1: Patentability Exclusions & Prior Art
- **Statutes:** Indian Patents Act 1970 Sec 3(p), 3(e), 10(4); 35 U.S.C. 101/102/103; EPC Art 54/56; JPO Part III.
- **Coverage:** Rejections based on traditional knowledge, synergistic combinations overcoming mere admixture, mandatory biological source disclosure.

### Domain 2: Trademarks & Brand Protection
- **Statutes:** Trade Marks Act 1999 Sec 9 & Sec 13; Nice Classification (Class 3 cosmetics, Class 5 pharmaceuticals, Class 30 herbal infusions).
- **Coverage:** Absolute prohibition against registering single generic Ayurvedic herb names (e.g. Ashwagandha, Triphala) as exclusive trademarks.

### Domain 3: Geographical Indications (GI)
- **Statutes:** Geographical Indications of Goods Act 1999.
- **Coverage:** Protecting regional traditional herbal products (e.g. Navara Rice, Darjeeling Tea, Alleppey Green Cardamom) against deceptive brand registration.

### Domain 4: Food Safety & Dietary Supplements
- **Statutes:** FSSAI (Ayurveda Aahara) Regulations 2022; US FDA DSHEA 1994; EU Directive 2002/46/EC.
- **Coverage:** Permitted dietary formulations, prohibited disease treatment claims, mandatory statutory disclaimers.

### Domain 5: Drugs & Cosmetics Regulations
- **Statutes:** Drugs & Cosmetics Act 1940, Rule 158B, Schedule T GMP.
- **Coverage:** Classical formulations (AFI/API cited) exempt from clinical trials vs. Ayurvedic Proprietary Medicines requiring safety and proof of effectiveness.

### Domain 6: Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS)
- **Statutes:** Biological Diversity Act 2002 (Sec 3, 4, 6); Nagoya Protocol.
- **Coverage:** Mandatory NBA approval prior to patent application filing in or outside India using Indian biological resources; benefit-sharing agreements.

### Domain 7: Traditional Knowledge Treatises (TK Prior Art)
- **Statutes:** Ayurvedic Formulary of India (AFI Parts I-III), Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia of India (API Parts I-II), TKDL Defensive Standard.
- **Coverage:** Classical slokas, standardized extract ratios, preparation methods acting as prior art against foreign biopiracy patents.

### Domain 8: Commercialization & D2C Licensing
- **Statutes:** AYUSH State Licensing Authority Guidelines, FSSAI Dual Licensing Framework.
- **Coverage:** Regulatory roadmap for launching wellness brands, avoiding misbranding penalties, dual-license optimization.

### Domain 9: Global Export & Monograph Standards
- **Statutes:** WHO Traditional Medicine Monographs; EU Directive 2004/24/EC; US FDA cGMP 21 CFR 111.
- **Coverage:** Heavy metal limits (Lead < 10 ppm, Cadmium < 0.3 ppm, Arsenic < 3 ppm, Mercury < 1 ppm), microbial load tolerances, stability testing for international export.

### Domain 10: International Patent Treaties
- **Statutes:** WIPO Treaty on Intellectual Property, Genetic Resources and Associated Traditional Knowledge (adopted May 2024); PCT Articles 19 & 34.
- **Coverage:** Mandatory international patent applicant disclosure of country of origin for genetic resources and indigenous traditional knowledge.
