# AYURLEX Jurisdiction-Specific Demo Questions
**Organized by Legal System and Sovereignty**  

---

## 1. National: India (`IN`)
1. `Q-IN-PAT-01`: Section 3(p) traditional knowledge exclusions and CSIR turmeric patent case.
2. `Q-IN-PAT-02`: Section 3(e) synergistic combinations vs mere admixtures.
3. `Q-IN-PAT-03`: Prior art extraction patents for standardized Withania somnifera.
4. `Q-IN-PAT-04`: Mandatory biological resource disclosure requirements under Section 10(4).
5. `Q-IN-TM-01`: Trade Marks Act Section 13 prohibition against registering herbal names.
6. `Q-IN-TM-02`: Nice classification (Classes 3, 5, 30) for Ayurvedic consumer goods.
7. `Q-IN-FSSAI-01`: FSSAI Ayurveda Aahara Regulations 2022 labeling and disease claim restrictions.
8. `Q-IN-FSSAI-02`: Regulatory boundary between FSSAI food supplements and AYUSH drugs.
9. `Q-IN-DCR-01`: Proof of safety and clinical efficacy requirements under Rule 158B.
10. `Q-IN-DCR-02`: Schedule T Good Manufacturing Practices mandatory requirements.
11. `Q-IN-ABS-01`: National Biodiversity Authority prior approval under Section 6 of BDA 2002.
12. `Q-IN-TK-01`: TKDL citations in patent examination against biopiracy.
13. `Q-IN-COMM-01`: AYUSH vs FSSAI licensing pathways for Ayurvedic D2C wellness brands.
14. `Q-IN-EXP-01`: Heavy metal limits (Lead, Cadmium, Arsenic, Mercury) under WHO standards.

## 2. National: United States (`US`)
1. `Q-US-PAT-01`: Granted USPTO claims for standardized Withania somnifera pharmaceutical extracts.
2. `Q-US-REG-01`: FDA DSHEA allowable structure-function claims vs unapproved drug claims.
3. `Q-US-PAT-02`: Overcoming 35 U.S.C. 101 natural product rejection for herbal compositions.

## 3. National: Japan (`JP`)
1. `Q-JP-PAT-01`: JPO patent claims for botanical compositions and extraction processes.
2. `Q-JP-PAT-02`: JPO Examination Guidelines for Kampo and traditional botanical medicines.

## 4. International: WIPO / PCT (`WO`)
1. `Q-WO-PCT-01`: PCT Article 33 international patentability requirements for botanical compositions.
2. `Q-WO-TK-01`: Mandatory disclosure of origin for genetic resources under the 2024 WIPO Treaty.

## 5. Regional: European Patent Office (`EP`)
1. `Q-EP-PAT-01`: EPC Article 54(5) second medical use claim drafting for botanical extracts.
2. `Q-EP-PAT-02`: Inventive step assessment (problem-solution approach) at the EPO for phytomedicines.

## 6. National: Germany (`DE`)
1. `Q-DE-AMG-01`: German Medicines Act (AMG §§ 39a-39d) traditional herbal medicinal product registration.
2. `Q-DE-PAT-01`: DPMA examination standards and BfArM monographs for phytopharmaceuticals.
