# Synergistic botanical composition comprising Boswellia serrata and Commiphora mukul for inflammatory joint diseases

**Patent ID:** `IN-324590-B` | **Country:** `INDIA` | **Language:** `en`
**Publication Date:** 2019-11-08 | **Filing Date:** 2011-03-24
**IPC:** A61K36/324, A61K36/328, A61P29/00, A61P19/02 | **CPC:** A61K36/324, A61K36/328
**Applicant:** National Botanical Research Institute (CSIR-NBRI)

---

## Abstract

A synergistic anti-arthritic oral composition combining standardized 3-O-acetyl-11-keto-beta-boswellic acid (AKBA >= 30%) with guggulsterones E and Z (>= 2.5%) from Commiphora mukul, significantly inhibiting joint swelling and pro-inflammatory TNF-alpha in adjuvant-induced arthritis models.

## Claims

### Claim 1 (Independent)
1. A synergistic oral botanical composition comprising: (a) a standardized extract of Boswellia serrata resin comprising at least 30.0% by weight of 3-O-acetyl-11-keto-beta-boswellic acid (AKBA); and (b) a standardized extract of Commiphora mukul gum resin comprising at least 2.5% by weight of combined guggulsterones E and Z; in a weight ratio of 2:1 to 4:1.

### Claim 2 (Dependent)
2. The composition as claimed in claim 1, wherein the combination produces greater than 70% reduction in paw edema in Freund's complete adjuvant arthritis models.

### Claim 3 (Dependent)
3. The composition as claimed in claim 1, wherein said composition is formulated into hard gelatin capsules.

## Description

Under Section 3(e) of the Indian Patents Act, evidence of synergy was submitted demonstrating that AKBA and guggulsterones act on complementary biological targets: 5-LOX inhibition and NF-kappa-B suppression respectively.
