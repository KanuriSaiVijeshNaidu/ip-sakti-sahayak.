# National Biodiversity Authority: Guidelines for Prior Approval under Section 6 of Biological Diversity Act

- **Source:** National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) - Statutory Body of Govt. of India
- **Authority:** National Biodiversity Authority (NBA), Chennai
- **Domain:** abs | **Subdomain:** nba_approvals
- **Jurisdiction:** IN | **Year:** 2014

## Statutory / Pharmacopoeial Text

Biological Diversity Act, 2002, Section 6(1) & NBA Guidelines.
Mandatory Statutory Requirement:
No person shall apply for any intellectual property right, by whatever name called, in or outside India for any invention based on any research or information on a biological resource obtained from India without obtaining the prior approval of the National Biodiversity Authority (NBA).

Form III Procedure & Timing:
1. Indian Applicants: May apply for patent first in India, but MUST obtain NBA approval before the grant of the patent.
2. Foreign Applicants / Entities with foreign equity: Must obtain prior approval before filing the patent application.
3. Application Form: Form III (Application for seeking prior approval of NBA for applying for intellectual property right).
4. Fee: Statutory fee of Rs. 500 per application.
5. Benefit Sharing Agreement: NBA executes an agreement stipulating benefit sharing (typically 0.2% to 1.0% of ex-factory gross sales if commercialized) into the National Biodiversity Fund.
