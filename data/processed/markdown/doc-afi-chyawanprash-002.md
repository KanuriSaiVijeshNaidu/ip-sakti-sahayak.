## Statutory / Pharmacopoeial Text

Ayurvedic Formulary of India (AFI), Part I, Formulation 3:11.
Canonical Formulation: Chyawanprash (Avaleha).
Classical Reference: Charaka Samhita, Chikitsa Sthana, Adhyaya 1:1, Sloka 62-74.
Key Ingredients: Fresh Amalaki fruits (500 pieces), decoction of Dashamoola and associated herbs (36 herbs), fried in cow ghee and sesame oil, enriched with sugar syrup, bamboo manna (Vamshalochana), long pepper (Pippali), cardamom (Ela), and raw honey.
Dosage & Therapeutic Use: 12 g to 24 g daily with milk. Indicated for Kasa (cough), Shwasa (dyspnoea), Kshaya (wasting), and Rasayana (immune rejuvenation).
Regulatory Distinction: Governed under the First Schedule of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940. Manufacturers marketing Chyawanprash under classical names cannot claim proprietary patent rights under Section 3(p) of the Patents Act, 1970.
