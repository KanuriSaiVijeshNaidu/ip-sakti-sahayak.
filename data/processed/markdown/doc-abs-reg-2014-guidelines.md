# Guidelines on Access to Biological Resources and Associated Knowledge and Benefits Sharing Regulations, 2014

## Statutory / Pharmacopoeial Text

Guidelines on Access to Biological Resources and Associated Knowledge and Benefits Sharing Regulations, 2014 (S.O. 3013(E)).
Regulation 9: Criteria for Benefit Sharing for Commercial Utilization of Biological Resources:
1. Option A (Based on purchase price of biological resource):
   - When buyer purchases directly from gatherers/cultivators: 1.0% to 3.0% of the purchase price.
   - When purchased through traders: 3.0% to 5.0% of the purchase price.
2. Option B (Based on annual gross ex-factory sale of product):
   - Up to Rupees 1 Crore: 0.1 per cent.
   - Rupees 1 Crore to 3 Crores: 0.2 per cent.
   - Above Rupees 3 Crores: 0.5 per cent.
3. IPR Commercialization: In case of patent transfer / licensing to a third party, the patentee must pay 3.0% to 5.0% of the royalty or licensing fee received.
