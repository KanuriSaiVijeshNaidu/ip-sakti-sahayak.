## Statutory / Pharmacopoeial Text

WHO International Standard Terminologies on Ayurveda (2023).
Concept ID: WHO-AYUR-0412 — Rasayana (रसायन / రసాయన / ரசாயனம்).
Definition: A specialized branch and pharmacological action in Ayurveda aimed at conserving, rejuvenating, and strengthening body tissues (Dhatus), promoting longevity, enhancing mental competence (Medha), and imparting disease resistance (Vyadhikshamatva).
Standardized Multilingual Equivalents:
- Sanskrit: Rasāyana (रसायन)
- English: Rejuvenation therapy / Adaptogenic bio-immunomodulation
- Telugu: రసాయనం (Rasayanamu)
- Tamil: ரசாயனம் (Rasayanam)
- Hindi: रसायन (Rasayan)
Regulatory Relevance: Claims on product labels regarding 'Rasayana' effects are classified under Ayurvedic drug pharmacology; under FSSAI Ayurveda Aahara regulations, label claims must restrict terminology to dietary wellness without alleging reversal of degenerative pathology.
