## Statutory / Pharmacopoeial Text

Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia of India (API), Part I, Vol I, Monograph 12.
Canonical Drug: Ashwagandha (Dry root of Withania somnifera Dunal, family Solanaceae).
Synonyms: Sanskrit: Hayagandha, Vajigandha, Balada; Hindi: Asgandh; Telugu: Asvagandha, Pennerugadda; Tamil: Amukkarakizhangu.
Macroscopic Characters: Roots straight, unbranched, conical, tortuous, 10 to 20 cm long, 10 to 20 mm thick, outer surface buff to grey-yellow with longitudinal wrinkles.
Identity & Quantitative Standards:
- Foreign Matter: Not more than 2 per cent.
- Total Ash: Not more than 7 per cent.
- Acid-insoluble Ash: Not more than 1 per cent.
- Alcohol-soluble Extractive: Not less than 15 per cent.
- Water-soluble Extractive: Not less than 19 per cent.
- Total Withanolides (Assay by HPLC): Not less than 0.20 per cent w/w calculated on dry root basis.
Regulatory Mandate: Under the Second Schedule to the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940, compliance with API monographs is legally mandatory for any licensed Ayurvedic drug manufacturer in India.
