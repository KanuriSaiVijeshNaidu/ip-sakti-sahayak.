## Statutory / Pharmacopoeial Text

Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia of India (API), Part I, Vol I, Monograph 24.
Canonical Drug: Guduchi (Dry stem of Tinospora cordifolia (Willd.) Miers, family Menispermaceae).
Synonyms: Sanskrit: Amrita, Chhinnaruha, Chakralakshana; Hindi: Giloy; Telugu: Tippateega; Tamil: Seenthilkodi.
Macroscopic Standards: Cylindrical pieces, 1 to 5 cm diameter, greenish-brown surface with prominent lenticels and longitudinal fissures; wheel-like vascular bundles in transverse section.
Physicochemical Parameters:
- Foreign Organic Matter: Not more than 2 per cent.
- Total Ash: Not more than 8 per cent.
- Acid-insoluble Ash: Not more than 0.5 per cent.
- Alcohol-soluble Extractive: Not less than 3 per cent.
- Water-soluble Extractive: Not less than 11 per cent.
- Bitters content / TLC Fingerprint matching marker compound cordifolioside A and tinocordiside.
Enforceability: Standard binding on all Ayurvedic formulations containing Giloy/Guduchi as per Section 33EEB of Drugs & Cosmetics Act 1940.
