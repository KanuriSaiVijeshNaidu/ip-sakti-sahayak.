# Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940: Section 33EE - Misbranded Ayurvedic Drugs

- **Source:** Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 (Act No. 23 of 1940)
- **Authority:** Central Drugs Standard Control Organization (CDSCO) & Ministry of Ayush
- **Domain:** ayush | **Subdomain:** drug_regulations
- **Jurisdiction:** IN | **Year:** 1940

## Statutory / Pharmacopoeial Text

Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940, Chapter IV-A, Section 33EE.
Statutory Definition of Misbranded Ayurvedic, Siddha or Unani Drugs:
An Ayurvedic drug shall be deemed to be misbranded:—
(a) if it is so coloured, coated, powdered or polished that damage is concealed, or if it is made to appear of better or greater therapeutic value than it really is;
(b) if it is not labelled in the prescribed manner; or
(c) if its label or container or anything accompanying the drug bears any statement, design or device which makes any false claim for the drug or which is false or misleading in any particular.

Regulatory Enforceability: Selling, stocking, or manufacturing a misbranded Ayurvedic drug is an offence punishable under Section 33-I with imprisonment and fine.
