- **Source:** Indian Patent Office (IP India) Official Journal

- **Domain:** patents | **Subdomain:** herbal_inventions

## Statutory / Pharmacopoeial Text

Indian Patent Number: IN 312450 (Application No. 201611012345).
Title: A Synergistic Standardized Phyto-Pharmaceutical Composition for Management of Glycemic Index.
Classification: IPC: A61K 36/9066, A61K 36/42, A61P 3/10.
Applicant: Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR).
Overcoming Section 3(e) Examination:
The Patent Office initially issued an objection under Section 3(e) alleging mere admixture of turmeric and bitter gourd.
Applicant's Evidence of Synergy:
- Quantitative isobologram analysis and Combination Index (CI) calculation.
- At an extract ratio of 3:2:0.5 (Curcuma : Momordica : Piperine), glucose uptake in L6 myotubes increased by 214%, compared to 78% for Curcuma alone and 62% for Momordica alone (CI = 0.48 < 1.0, establishing strong pharmacological synergism).
Claim 1 (Granted): A synergistic oral composition comprising 45-55% w/w standardized curcuminoids, 30-40% charantin-enriched fraction, and 5% piperine, demonstrating Combination Index < 0.6.
