# Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945: Rule 158B - Guidelines for Issue of License with Respect to ASU Drugs

- **Source:** Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945

## Statutory / Pharmacopoeial Text

Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945, Part XVI, Rule 158B.
Regulatory Mandate for Patent or Proprietary ASU Medicines:
For the grant of a manufacturing license (Form 25D) for Ayurvedic Patent or Proprietary medicines, applicants must submit:
Category A (Classical ingredients with new indication or dosage):
- Published literature / textual references from First Schedule texts.
- Acute oral toxicity data.
Category B (New combination of classical ingredients):
- Safety data: Acute oral toxicity studies as per OECD guidelines.
- Proof of Efficacy: Evidence from published peer-reviewed journals or a pilot clinical trial conducted by a recognized clinical research organization.
Category C (Aqueous/hydroalcoholic extracts of classical ingredients):
- Heavy metal testing, pesticide residue testing, microbial load compliance, and batch standardization data.
