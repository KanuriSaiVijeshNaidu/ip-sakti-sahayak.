# Ayurvedic Formulary of India: Triphala Churna Specification

- **Source:** Ayurvedic Formulary of India (AFI)
- **Authority:** Ministry of Ayush / Pharmacopoeia Commission
- **Domain:** ayush | **Subdomain:** classical_formulations
- **Jurisdiction:** IN | **Year:** 2003

## Statutory / Pharmacopoeial Text

Ayurvedic Formulary of India (AFI), Part I, Formulation 7:13.
Canonical Formulation: Triphala Churna.
Classical Reference: Sharangadhara Samhita, Madhyama Khanda, Adhyaya 6: 9-11.
Formulation Composition:
1. Haritaki fruit pericarp (Terminalia chebula Retz.) - 1 Part (Equal proportion).
2. Bibhitaki fruit pericarp (Terminalia bellirica Roxb.) - 1 Part (Equal proportion).
3. Amalaki fruit pericarp (Phyllanthus emblica L.) - 1 Part (Equal proportion).
Method of Preparation: De-seeded pericarps of mature fruits are dried, individually pulverized to fine powder (passing through mesh sieve 80), and blended uniformly in equal parts.
Dosage Form & Dosage: Churna (fine powder), 3 g to 6 g once or twice daily with warm water or honey.
Indications: Vibandha (constipation), Deepana (carminative), Netraroga (eye disorders), and Rasayana (antioxidant tonic).
Patent Law Relevance: Under Section 3(e) of the Indian Patents Act, any patent application seeking protection for an equal-ratio combination of Haritaki, Bibhitaki, and Amalaki is rejected as an unpatentable mere admixture of traditionally known ingredients unless an inventive extraction step and synergistic pharmacological effect is proven.
